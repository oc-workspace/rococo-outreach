## Security notes

- Do not commit SMTP passwords, authorization codes, bearer tokens, cookies, or full recipient addresses.
- Do not treat SMTP authentication as proof that Sent-folder synchronization works; the latter was confirmed manually for this controlled test.
- Do not add automatic port failover during a send, because a retry on another port could create duplicate mail.
